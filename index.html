Open datasets by JasonTKG

Each dataset below is the data behind a reference site. The figures are read from the official or openly licensed sources named in the dataset's README, checked by program, and released as CSV or JSON files with a stable download address. To report an error, open an issue on the dataset's repository.

Dog breed lifespan figures from four open-access studies

Median lifespan and life expectancy by dog breed, as published in four open-access (CC BY 4.0) studies, one row per study and breed.

Site: dog-breed-data.at01.io · Data: github.com/JasonTKG/dog-breed-data · latest release · CC BY 4.0

The Psalms verse by verse in three public-domain English texts

All 150 psalms, verse by verse, in the King James Version, the American Standard Version and the World English Bible, with a per-psalm index.

Site: psalms-and-prayers.at01.io · Data: github.com/JasonTKG/psalms-and-prayers · latest release · CC0 1.0

Dates of holidays observed in the United States, by year

Computed dates of 35 federal, Christian, Jewish and other holidays observed in the United States, each confirmed against a published official table.

Site: holiday-dates.at01.io · Data: github.com/JasonTKG/us-holiday-dates · latest release · CC BY 4.0

USDA PLANTS characteristics for 534 plants, one row per plant

Conservation plant characteristics, native status and distribution counts for 534 plants from the USDA PLANTS Database, with the hardiness zone band of each plant's minimum temperature.

Site: plant-data-sheets.at01.io · Data: github.com/JasonTKG/plant-data-sheets · latest release · CC0 1.0

Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023: sections, IPC correspondence and classification

The 358 sections of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 as enacted (Gazette of India), the government's IPC correspondence table and the BNSS First Schedule classification.

Site: bns.govsimple.org · Data: github.com/JasonTKG/bns-sections · latest release · CC0 1.0

Fiestas laborales locales, autonómicas y nacionales de España, 2026 y 2027

Fiestas locales de 2027 de 1.167 municipios de Andalucía, Galicia y Canarias y fiestas nacionales y autonómicas de 2026 y 2027, leídas de los boletines oficiales.

Site: festivos.govsimple.org · Data: github.com/JasonTKG/calendario-festivos-locales · latest release · CC0 1.0

SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG contribution rules, withholding and income tax tables, pay multipliers and regional minimum wages, each tied to the official issuance it comes from.
